Our Story

An Oklahoma tradition, griddled fresh in La Mesa

The onion burger got its start in Oklahoma during the Depression, when cooks stretched a little ground beef further by smashing it thin over a bed of sliced onions right on the flat-top. The onions caramelize into the meat as it cooks, so every bite carries that char and sweetness through the whole patty.

We still build ours the same way — fresh ground Angus beef, thin-sliced onions, a hot griddle, and no shortcuts. Fries are cut from whole potatoes and double-fried daily, and every milkshake is hand-dipped to order, one cup at a time.

  • Fresh ground Angus beef, never frozen
  • Onions cooked directly into every patty
  • Potatoes cut and double-fried fresh, daily
  • Milkshakes hand-dipped to order
